
# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the StellarSettle community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Showing empathy and respect toward other contributors.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional community.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit or remove contributions that violate this Code of Conduct and take appropriate corrective action.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all StellarSettle community spaces and situations where someone officially represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project's designated enforcement contact:

**Contact:** To be confirmed by the project maintainer before merging.

Reports should be reviewed promptly and fairly, with appropriate care for the privacy of everyone involved.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers should apply proportionate responses based on the severity and impact of a violation. Responses may include a private correction, a warning, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban.
